GenoScreen Genetic Variant Exploration Prototype

Biomedical Engineering Project

Explore HEXA Variant Information

GenoScreen is an educational prototype that demonstrates how genetic variant information may be organized for preliminary review. It does not diagnose or rule out Tay-Sachs disease.

Important: This demo does not connect to a validated clinical database or run a trained machine-learning model. Do not enter personal, identifiable, or real patient information. Results are for education only.

Educational summary

Project objectives

  • Introduce the HEXA gene and its relationship to Tay-Sachs disease.
  • Demonstrate a simple interface for recording variant annotations.
  • Encourage careful interpretation and review by qualified professionals.

Future improvements

  • Connect to a verified variant database and show source citations.
  • Integrate a documented, validated model with reproducible evaluation.
  • Add privacy controls, clinician review, and appropriate validation before any real-world use.
